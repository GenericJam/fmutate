POT vs ENCODER

{{k.label}}

Pot: arc with two ends, a value. Encoder: closed ring, no ends; ticks light as clicks are sent. Drag up or right for more; hold still 0.4 s first for fine (¼ speed). Knobs are optional; sliders are the default.
