A finger belongs to one control

Whatever it lands on owns it until it lifts, even after it slides away. Finger 2 grabs its own control, so two-knob DJ moves just work.

Knobs: up or right is more

Movement = right + up − left − down, so diagonals just work. Touching never jumps the value. Pot: 0–100 over ~200 pt, haptic bump at each end. Encoder: 1 click per 6 pt.

Hold still, then drag, for fine

Works for knobs, sliders and XY pads, one-handed, at any edge. Only that control changes; your other hand stays free to play.

Out of room? Lift and re-grab

Every drag is relative, like a trackpad, so re-grabbing never jumps. No control sits in the 28 pt edge gutter.

Sliders: the default

Work anywhere on screen. They move from where you land, so a stray tap never jumps. Jump-to-position is an option for MIDI CCs only. Bound to an encoder, a slider sends clicks.

XY pad: two inputs, one finger

Each axis is bound separately; diagonal moves change both. Relative like a slider. Spring back sends the clicks in reverse, for a wobble that returns home.

Keys, pads, strum: every finger plays

The exception to rule 1. Sliding onto the next key plays it (glissando, strum). With wobble on, sliding sideways bends the held note instead. Empty panel space does nothing.
