# FMutate privacy policy

FMutate collects no personal data.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.** The app has no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or crash
  reporting service. Nothing about you or how you use the app is sent anywhere.
- **Network requests** only fetch things the app shows or plays (the firmware list, firmware packages and
  cartridges, the firmwares' READMEs and pictures), from The Firmware Hub (thefirmwarehub.com), GitHub
  (github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com) and GitHub Pages (genericjam.github.io). These are ordinary downloads:
  like any web request, the server sees your IP address, under that site's own privacy policy. FMutate sends no
  identifier or other information about you with them.
- **On the device only**: your layouts, saved patches, settings and downloaded firmwares stay on your phone. MIDI
  and Bluetooth MIDI controllers are used only to play the synth on the device.
- **Permissions**: Bluetooth (MIDI controllers) and motion sensors (motion controls you bind in the editor) are
  used on the device and never recorded or sent. FMutate doesn't use the camera, microphone or photo library; iOS
  lists those permission texts because the app's framework contains code for them.
